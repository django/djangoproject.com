Strengthening Django: The Django Fellows

Our paid Fellows are critical to Django. While the vast majority of Django contributions come from unpaid volunteers, sustaining and growing the paid DSF Django Fellowship program means:

  • 🔒 Handling security reports swiftly.
  • ⚡ Speeding up the design of new features.
  • 🫶 Supporting the Django package ecosystem.
  • ⭐️ Leading mentorship programs and developer sprints.

To make Django more sustainable and responsive, we are expanding the Fellowship program with a new Sponsored Fellowship initiative. This lets the DSF increase the number of Fellows, get more work done, and ensure community priorities are addressed with direct feedback from top-level sponsors.

Our goal is clear: faster pull request reviews, quicker security responses, and a healthier ecosystem for Django developers and package maintainers. This expansion is essential to keep up with Django’s growth and ensure that contributors, especially volunteers, are supported and not blocked.

Django Powers the Web for Perfectionists With Deadlines. The DSF Powers Django.

Django is an open source web framework trusted by companies, organizations, and governments worldwide to build everything from content management systems to social networks to scientific computing platforms. It’s reliable, secure, scalable, ridiculously fast, and free to use for everyone.

With 87.6k GitHub stars, 34k forks, and an estimated 500,000 to 1 million projects built on it globally, Django powers critical infrastructure for thousands of companies and tens of thousands of developers. Yet, despite its widespread adoption and impact, the DSF has historically received only around $250,000 per year to sustain Django.

This stark contrast underscores a hard truth: open-source success doesn't guarantee sustainability and that is precisely why Django needs your support. By sponsoring Django, you’re not just giving back. You’re investing in the future of reliable, secure, and innovative web development.

As a sponsor, your organization also gains global recognition and aligns your brand with one of the most respected frameworks in the open-source world. Support Django. Help it thrive.

Our Mission

Sustain Django. Support its Community. Strengthen its Future.

The DSF is a community-driven 501(c)(3) non-profit dedicated to supporting, promoting, protecting, and advancing the Django Web Framework.

Our mission is to ensure Django remains and grows as a tool enabling robust, accessible, and inclusive web applications built by developers worldwide. We do this by:

  • Empowering Contributor Participation: Support development of Django by sponsoring sprints, meetups, gatherings and community events.
  • Expanding Outreach: Invest in outreach to encourage Django’s use in education, startups, and enterprises to make Django more visible and more valuable.
  • Investing in Long Term Stability: Funding folks to strategically lead aspects on technical development and business operations.
  • Providing Financial Aid: Offer grants and aid so contributors across the world can attend conferences and engage with the community.
  • Covering Infrastructure Cost: Maintain system infrastructure and hosting fees which is the backbone of reliable open-source projects.
  • Fostering Global Community: Ensure support goes beyond borders and actively welcomes individuals from all backgrounds.

Supporting the Global Django Community

Educational Outreach

The DSF provides funding for local groups around the world, including to Django Girls workshops. Django Girls workshops are free, volunteer-run events to introduce primarily women to Python and Django. Attendees learn valuable skills to build their first web app with guidance from mentors. Many attendees become community leaders who go on to organize their own workshops, help others learn to code, and secure full-time jobs as developers.

To help these workshops and similar groups thrive, the DSF offers small grants to cover essential costs like reserving locations with internet access for people to come together and learn. All groups are welcome to apply for this funding, and the DSF prioritizes funding communities that struggle to find ways to cover the costs through other means.

Conference and Event Support

The DSF supports Django conferences and events around the world, sometimes in partnership with other nonprofits such as the Django Events Foundation North America (DEFNA). The DSF also supports Python conferences with Django presence. The DSF prioritizes grant programs that make attending events more accessible and inclusive for those facing financial barriers.
