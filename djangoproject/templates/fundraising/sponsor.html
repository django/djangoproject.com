Find your starting point

Consider your company’s size, how much you rely on Django, and the annual support you can sustain.

  • Larger organizations investing in Django’s future: consider Diamond for leadership connections, or Sponsored Fellow for direct engagement with the people maintaining Django.
  • Growing companies building on Django: explore Gold for newsletter and blog opportunities, or Platinum for more frequent promotion and wider website visibility.
  • Small teams starting to give back: choose Bronze for core recognition, or Silver to share company news throughout the year.

Any company can choose any tier. For a short campaign, explore weekly or monthly banners. For help choosing, talk to the Foundation.
