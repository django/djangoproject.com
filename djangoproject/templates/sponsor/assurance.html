Django Assurance proposes a signed, renewable agreement around Django’s published release and security policy. Give your engineering investment a place in your procurement process while funding the people maintaining Django.

This program is a proposed pilot. Agreement terms and availability are still being developed. Contact the Foundation to discuss your requirements.

Built around Django’s existing commitments

A commitment on file

The proposal gives organizations a signed agreement about maintaining Django itself, for use in internal procurement and dependency reviews.

Plan around published support windows

Django’s LTS policy provides security and data-loss fixes, typically for three years. Coverage depends on the version’s published support window.

Read Django’s release policy →

One agreement, annual invoicing

The proposed program supports annual renewal and discussion of multi-year commitments, funding the Foundation and its Fellowship program.

Framework maintenance

The proposed agreement covers Django’s release and security policy. It does not include support for your application code or response-time SLAs for your tickets.
